Selected work

Searching a cell atlas with words

I’m building tools with FlyBase that match gene-expression profiles to descriptions of fruit fly cells. A researcher can start with a cell type or tissue and find cells whose expression fits that description.

Cell–language model code

AI agents for biological data analysis

We built SCAPE to test whether agents choose sound analysis steps and reach defensible conclusions from single-cell data. In related work, we use workflows from earlier studies to guide cell annotation and protein analysis on new datasets.

SCAPE paper SCAPE poster acceptance · ML4Molecules at NeurIPS 2026

AI agents for scientific research

I helped build a system that takes a researcher’s hypothesis through literature review, mathematical analysis, and experiments. In a separate survey, we examined what evidence can support the claims such systems produce.

pAI/MSc preprint Code Peer-reviewed survey · AI for Science Workshop at ICML 2026

How should an AI system spend its reasoning time?

With a fixed compute budget, I compare one model revising its answer with several models voting or debating. I track when a correct answer first appears and whether the system ultimately selects it.

Why the choice of optimizer matters

We studied how Muon reshapes weight matrices during training. It outperformed AdamW on a small language model, but the ranking reversed on a small vision transformer.

Muon preprint

Social experience and the aging brain

I use genetics and brain MRI to study social isolation and differences in white matter. With single-nucleus RNA data, I also ask how childhood adversity relates to gene expression in the aging human brain.

Imaging genetics code Related MIT project on personality and gene regulation

Early environments and behavior

I analyze movement patterns in mother mice and their offspring to study how behavior varies with social isolation, environmental enrichment, and limited nesting materials.

Behavior analysis code

Mobile vision assessment

At OHSU, I help develop a near-vision assessment app for people with presbyopia and a screening app for early signs of nearsightedness in children.
