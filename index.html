Learning theory

2026 · Preprint

The spectral dynamics and noise geometry of Muon

Why does the same optimizer help one model and hurt another? We studied how Muon reshapes weight matrices during training. In our experiments, Muon beat AdamW on a small language model, while AdamW performed better on a small vision transformer.

With Pierfrancesco Beneventano and Tomaso Poggio

Computational neuroscience

2024–2025 · MIT SuperUROP

Personality, environment, and gene expression in the brain

How do experiences such as social isolation relate to molecular differences in the human brain? My SuperUROP project asked how genetic data and single-nucleus measurements from the prefrontal cortex could help connect personality and environmental exposures to gene regulation.

Research interests

What can AI contribute to a scientific experiment?

I’m interested in how a scientist can check an agent’s reasoning, reproduce its analysis, and decide which of its conclusions to pursue.

What determines what a neural network learns?

I use theory and small, controlled experiments to study how the choice of optimizer changes the representations a network learns.

What can biological intelligence teach us about AI?

I want to connect explanations of intelligence across scales, from gene expression and neural activity to perception and behavior. I’m especially interested in which principles of human learning could inform artificial systems.
