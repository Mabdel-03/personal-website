FlyBase Single Cell Analysis Platform

We built tools for FlyBase curators to process fly single-cell datasets and trace answers about a cell population back to its gene expression.

Motivation

Fly researchers publish single-cell measurements from different tissues, ages, and experiments. Before a curator can compare two studies, they have to decide which cells to keep and how cell populations correspond. We wanted to automate the repeatable steps and let curators inspect the evidence behind a proposed annotation.

Prior work

FlyBase brings together gene records, published experiments, and a shared vocabulary for fly biology. The Fly Cell Atlas and Aging Fly Cell Atlas add expression profiles and cell-type annotations across tissues. Together they provide a reference for asking whether a proposed cell identity fits what researchers have already observed.

What we delivered

We delivered prototypes for internal testing with the FlyBase collaboration. The processing tools check data quality, group cells, and propose annotations using marker genes that curators can review. The investigation tools let researchers ask about a population in plain language, call the relevant analysis, and trace the resulting genes and figures back to the data.
